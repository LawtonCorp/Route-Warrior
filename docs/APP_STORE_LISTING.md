## Description

You know your streets. The nav app knows its algorithm. Route Rebel
settles the argument.

Drive like you always do — Route Rebel records your actual route
hands-free and snapshots what Google Maps planned at the moment you left.
Then it answers the questions you've argued about at dinner:

- How long did it really take vs. the predicted ETA?
- How many stop signs and traffic lights are on my route?
- How was traffic — and how wrong was the prediction?
- When is my way faster: 7:30 on a Tuesday? Friday afternoon?
- Bottom line: should I follow the directions, or trust myself?

**Race your own ghost.** On a route you've driven before, the lock screen
shows a live ahead/behind timer against your personal best. Beat yesterday.

**Private by design.** No accounts. No servers of ours. No analytics. Your
driving history lives on your iPhone and in your private iCloud — nowhere
else. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is the route request that
makes the comparison possible.

**Route Rebel Pro** puts Google's plan on the scoreboard next to Apple's,
opens every drive you have ever recorded and every destination, adds the
heatmaps, trends and route races, the live drive view with turn-by-turn
directions and reroute, and the ghost race. Recording is always free —
your data keeps building from day one.

Your commute has a leaderboard now.
